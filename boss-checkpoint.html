Boss Checkpoint

Each grade has a boss guarding the checkpoint. The battle mixes grammar, vocabulary and listening from that level: 12 questions, and the boss has 10 HP. Every right answer is a hit. Every wrong answer costs you a shield. You have 3 shields.

Beat the boss and you earn that level's badge. It's saved on this device and appears in your Mission Log. Listening questions read a sentence aloud, so switch your sound on (or grab headphones in class).
